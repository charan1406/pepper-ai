A. Clean light — engineering tool

Light background, minimal borders, no glow effects. Feels like a proper robotics lab tool — Figma/CAD aesthetic. White panels, subtle shadows, warm neutral tones. The 3D scene can stay darker (it's a 3D viewport) but all UI is light.

B. Warm dark — macOS/iOS aesthetic

Dark but warm, like macOS dark mode. No blue/purple hue, no glows. Neutral grays (#2c2c2e, #3a3a3c), green accents for positive states, white text. Subtle borders instead of shadows. Feels native, not "AI-generated".

C. Zinc dark — Tailwind/Vercel aesthetic

Dark zinc palette (no blue/purple cast), green for positive states, red for warnings. Clean borders, zero glow. Like Vercel dashboard or Linear app. Modern but restrained.
